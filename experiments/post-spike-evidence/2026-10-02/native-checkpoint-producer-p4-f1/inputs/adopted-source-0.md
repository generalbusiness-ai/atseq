# Let a genuine bridge free witness capacity

The 64-witness cap must allow the read that can resolve those witnesses. At capacity, the existing private owner may verify a selected head at or above its highest unbridged witness through a genuine continuous chain from its accepted prefix. Acceptance commits the new prefix and compaction of exactly covered witnesses in **one cursor transition**. A non-bridging read that would add another independent witness is refused when capacity is unavailable. No witness is evicted to make room.

This clarifies J-1 in independent report `379456884a324985a74e2127a380894d65dc2c5b`. The [original J1 packet](../experiments/post-spike-evidence/2026-10-02/materialization-checkpoint-j1/inputs/j1-original/contract.json), every original full P3/P4 obligation and all **39 + 44 + 20 original runtime vectors** remain retained and **UNEXECUTED**. Three additional [bridge schedules](../experiments/post-spike-evidence/2026-10-02/materialization-checkpoint-j1/unexecuted-vectors.json) are also **UNEXECUTED**. This is a source proposal, not implemented bridge verification, storage recovery or task completion.

Use the same checked R1 chain route, app/genesis scope, selected genuine P1 root and captured existing owner. Capture the accepted prefix, all unbridged witnesses, highest pair, cursor and applicable binding/source/floor facts before awaits. The bridge route must verify each positional original entry, signature and predecessor from the accepted prefix through the selected head, and compare every covered witness's **exact entry CID** and retained proof/method context. Existing proof/cache/path/delta/time/storage budgets apply; none is raised. An authenticated higher head alone, caller flag, claimed bridge or unrelated app's history cannot choose this acceptance route or free capacity.

After every relevant await, recheck captured owner/current generation and known floors/cursor as already required. Build the candidate prefix and resulting unbridged witness set together. Compact only witnesses actually bridged by the checked chain; retain every remaining witness and negative fact, including participant/other-app facts in their existing owner scopes. The resulting retained references and all rows, proofs, keys, versions and metadata must fit the existing count/reference/store policies. A concurrent new witness cannot be silently forgotten to make a captured bridge fit. A stale candidate withholds acceptance and follows the existing known-floor/uncertainty rules.

Commit prefix rows, metadata, floors and exactly covered witness compaction atomically under the same expected cursor. Confirm transaction completion and the final owner checks before exposing the prefix or acknowledging/appending. Do not delete witnesses first, accept prefix separately, split this acceptance across cursors or downgrade origin/assurance. This is the proposed durable integration of existing genuine owners; it creates no tree, index, proof, identity, storage or permission owner.

If the bridge transition aborts, is uncertain or completes late against stale memory/floor, retain the checked knowledge and witnesses in memory, withhold acknowledgment/append and poison the continuation until checked reconciliation. A confirmed abort leaves the old committed witness set intact. An uncertain result cannot assume either the new prefix or compacted list was committed. Checked restart reads actual durable rows and reconciles durable pending evidence; it cannot recover an uncommitted higher fact by assertion. These are the same durable-preservation limits as the original design, not a new guarantee. Missing/invalid bridge evidence cannot compact witnesses; observed faults keep their existing precedence.
