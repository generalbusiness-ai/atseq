## Adopted decisions and current recommendations

- The [incremental reader](2026-10-01-atseq-incremental-reader-requirements.md)
  uses account revisions as advisory cursors and preserves every known app floor.
  A verified current boundary is distinct from an interior-history audit.
- The [native wire contract](2026-10-01-atseq-native-wire-contract.md) uses native
  app publication, exact retained bytes and explicit account/device/control roles.
  Recovery appointments outrank governance changes. Recovery-only appointments
  avoid mixed-power map pressure; one compromised recovery key can still replace
  the recovery set. No quorum or new bypass operation is claimed.
- The [materialization design](2026-10-01-atseq-materialized-checkpoints.md) and
  [checkpoint bytes](2026-10-01-atseq-checkpoint-policy-bytes.md) distinguish trusted
  atomic local restore, portable app assertions and independent genesis replay.
  Complete/deferred prior-index mode is local configuration. Version 1 implies
  native publication; history determines the pending tail. Closed row schemas,
  literal native vectors and full implementation remain joint gates. Selective
  local outcome rows use actual positions and cannot be exported as complete.
- The [enrolment design](2026-10-01-atseq-account-enrolment-design.md) uses atomic
  first epoch/current/grant creation, narrow collection scopes and separate key
  purposes. The [OAuth review](2026-10-01-atseq-oauth-client-review-disposition.md)
  adopts both official clients with six conditions. Credential custody lives on
  a separate enrolment/session origin; app-supplied views cannot run there. Lazy
  import and guarded network/callback/lock boundaries still need executable
  evidence. Nonextractable keys do not secure a compromised origin or session.
- [Complete state remains the default](2026-10-01-atseq-effects-transaction-options.md)
  with V0 and checkpoints. Effects need a concrete beyond-cap application and
  actual at-cap measurements before a prototype. The published V0 proposal keeps
  its original eight-option evidence; shipped V0 measures the actual validation
  kernel, without claiming an end-to-end native speedup.
